How it works

1

Pick the apps to lock

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, whatever pulls you in. Your choice stays private on your device.

2

Go for a run

Every mile earns time. Walks, rides, strength and yoga all count too, synced automatically from Apple Health.

3

Earn your minutes

Every mile you run adds app time. No camera, no counting reps, no manual logging.

4

Unlock, then lock again

Tap a locked app and spend earned minutes to unlock. It locks again when your time is up.

Fair by design

🏃

Runs and every workout earn

Your runs and workouts from Garmin, Strava, Apple Watch, COROS and Polar all sync through Apple Health.

🎯

Goals & rewards

Set distance, workout or screen time goals and pick your own reward for hitting them.

🚫

No cheating

Manually entered workouts do not count, because you have to actually move.

🎛️

Your rules

Set your own earning rate for runs and other workouts to match your goals.

Private by design

No account. No backend. Your runs, your balance, and the apps you lock never leave your device. We read workout data from Apple Health only to calculate your earned minutes. It stays on your device, never uploaded, never sold. Read our Privacy Policy.
